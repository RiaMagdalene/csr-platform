# ATLAS — CSR Intelligence Platform

> **Direct capital where it matters.**

ATLAS is a CSR intelligence platform designed to make corporate social investment more targeted, explainable, and locally relevant.

The platform maps district-level development needs, identifies opportunity gaps, and matches priority districts with NGOs based on **geography, sector alignment, CSR budget, and organisational capacity**.

## ✨ Core Features

### Discover

Explore district-level development signals across the Tamil Nadu pilot, including need intensity and CSR allocation.

### Prioritize

Identify areas where **high development need and relatively lower CSR allocation** create greater opportunity for targeted intervention.

### Plan

Match priority districts with suitable NGOs using an explainable scoring model based on:

* Geography
* Sector alignment
* CSR budget
* Organisational capacity

### Track

ATLAS provides a foundation for future impact tracking through personalized impact goals, watchlists, and portfolio-level CSR planning.

## 🧮 Explainable Matching

ATLAS uses a transparent weighted scoring model:

| Factor    | Weight |
| --------- | -----: |
| Geography |    30% |
| Sector    |    30% |
| Budget    |    20% |
| Capacity  |    20% |

This allows CSR teams to understand **why an organisation is recommended**, rather than relying on an opaque ranking.

## 📊 Opportunity Gap

ATLAS combines district-level need with CSR allocation to highlight areas where additional intervention may have greater potential impact.

The platform uses an opportunity-gap approach based on:

**Need × relative CSR under-allocation**

This helps shift CSR planning from simply identifying high-need areas toward identifying **high-need areas with comparatively lower existing CSR allocation**.
